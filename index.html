04 / The artist

Space.
Groove.
Instinct.

Swaylo P is a DJ, producer and label founder whose sound grows from Bucharest’s underground electronic culture.

Working between rominimal, microhouse, deep tech and electro, he approaches a set as a gradual conversation with the room. Detailed rhythms, shifts in texture and the freedom of an extended session sit at the centre of his selections.

His catalogue includes Forever Loving on Rhythmic Steps, the Root collaboration “Paramount” on Saraw, “Crush Out” on Go Spastik and Funky Way on deeperdub. Through Aievea Records, he also develops his own releases alongside music from other producers.
