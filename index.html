Bio

From Valea Jiului, Petrosani, formed by the underground scene in Bucharest, Swaylo P has evolved from a passionate selector into a mature DJ and producer, with a clear artistic direction.

In the last year he mixed wherever he had the opportunity, gradually building his experience at the desk and refining his sound through constant appearances. Known for precise groove control and coherent flow, his sets sit in a modern club language where minimal, deep tech and dancefloor rhythms meet naturally.

He also built real club stamina through an almost year-long residency at Emerald in Bucharest’s Old Town, playing every night of every weekend, a chapter that consolidated his crowd reading, pacing, and long-form flow.

In parallel with his artistic path, Patrik founded his own company to share his experience further, offering DJ and music production courses for the new generation of artists. During the day he works in the technical field, and at night he builds dancefloors.

This appearance on Radio Deea marks a new chapter: a set of 100% original productions, designed to present in a single statement both his identity as a performer and his vision as a producer.

Looking ahead, Swaylo P is expanding his reach with a selective fly-in approach, pushing his sound into afterhours ecosystems and festival stages, while preparing a dedicated studio and label rooted in creative freedom and education.
