Bio

Born in Valea Jiului and shaped by the underground culture of Bucharest, Swaylo P is a DJ and producer with a clear artistic direction rooted in groove, control, and long-form storytelling.

His evolution came through constant club exposure, building experience night after night and refining his sound through real dancefloor interaction. His sets sit in a modern club language where minimal, deep tech, and driving dancefloor rhythms connect naturally, with a strong focus on flow rather than peak-chasing.

He developed his identity through extended club sessions, sharpening crowd reading, pacing, endurance, and clean low-end discipline across real dancefloor environments.

Alongside his DJ work, Swaylo P is actively producing original music designed for club and afterhours systems, releasing material internationally and preparing a wider body of work for selective showcases, labels, and promoters.

Today, he operates with a selective fly-in approach, focusing on afterhours environments, curated club lineups, and festival stages where long-form vision and musical patience are valued over trends.
